Energetic Speaking Tip

Sounding Friendly & Confident

When meeting someone for the first time, your pitch should have a warm rise at the end of friendly greetings!

Intonation Curve: Rise ↗ on "meet you"
Key Conversation Blocks
  • What's your name? Ask
  • My name is [Name] Answer
  • Nice to meet you! Greet
  • Do you want to...? Invite
III

Language in Use

Core Grammar Patterns
1 كيف ترحب بالآخرين وتسأل عن أسمائهم

How to welcome others and ask about their names

Question Pattern:
Hello! What's your name?
للسؤال عن الاسم نستخدم ⬅️ ما اسمك ؟
Answer Options:
1. Hello! My name is + [name]
2. Hello! I'm + [name]
للإجابة ⬅️
Note: What's = What is (Contraction for fluent speech)
2 عند مقابلة شخص لأول مرة

Meeting someone for the first time

👱‍♀️
Person A:
"Nice to meet you, Hana."
يسعدني لقاؤكِ يا هنا.
👧
Person B:
"Nice to meet you, too."
يسعدني لقاؤكِ أيضًا.
💡 Pro Tip: Always add "too" at the end when responding back!
3 هل تريد أن... ؟

Do you want to _________ ?

Invitation Phrase:
Do you want to shake hands?
هل تريدين أن نتصافح ؟
Happy Confirmation:
"Yes!" or "Yes, let's play!"
نعم !
More verbs: Do you want to play? / Do you want to practice?
